## Reading the numbers

* Converting the object with `nurbs_float` is the single most important optimisation: exact evaluation
  keeps `sqrt(2)` symbolic and simplifies at every step (30x slower for curvatures).
* `nurbs_compile()` translates the span search and basis-function kernels to Lisp and compiles them.
  The gain is largest where those kernels dominate (curve points) and small where the cost is in
  list arithmetic and simplification (surface derivatives, curvatures).
* bfloat arithmetic is 5-10x slower than float; it is meant for ill-conditioned evaluations near
  singular points, not for meshing.
* geomdl is 20-60x faster on float evaluation. NURBS-Maxima is not a meshing engine: its range is
  exact/symbolic analysis, arbitrary precision, mathematical prototyping, teaching, and moderate
  meshes (10^3-10^4 points).
